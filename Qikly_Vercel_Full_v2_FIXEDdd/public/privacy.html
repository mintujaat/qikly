Privacy Policy

Qikly may collect Telegram username, Telegram User ID, subscription information, order identifiers and payment status to provide access and customer support. Payment credentials are processed by Razorpay and are not stored by Qikly as card/UPI credentials. Firebase may store operational data required for subscriptions and administration. Local storage may remember the age-gate acknowledgement and limited subscription UI information. We do not intentionally collect information from people under 18. Contact the operator for questions or data requests.
