Privacy Policy

Qikly collects account, contact, delivery and order information needed to operate the storefront, process orders, provide support and improve the shopping experience.

Payment information

Online payments are handled by the configured payment gateway. Qikly stores order/payment references required for verification and customer support, not the customer's card or UPI secret.

Images and uploads

Product and storefront images uploaded by administrators may be stored using the configured ImgBB integration.

Cookies and local storage

The storefront may use cookies for login sessions and local browser storage for cart, wishlist, compare and recent-shopping preferences.
